# eitri@44745c2

Current offline economy laboratory. This supersedes `eitri@72e2765` and folds
in the subsequent deep-search portfolios through `a00f798` and `44745c2`.
It is documented here for reproducibility but is deliberately **not submitted
to either ladderfarm queue**: standalone Eitri's earlier online verdict showed
that pure economy is not a competitive promotion candidate.

## Scope

Only seat-specific deposit insertion-order data changed. The modular planner,
one-to-four-builder assignment, and executor are unchanged. A deterministic
10,000-permutation search targeted maps still below their terrain-flow ceiling.
Every winning model proposal was then compared with its exact parent in the
actual game engine; modeled regressions were rejected.

The final added portfolios cover Bifrost, Eider, Frostgate, Nordkap, Heart,
Snowflake, Yggdrasil, Glacierkeep, Icefloe, and Paths. The last search pass adds
13,230 Ti by round 1000, led by Paths (+7,750 seat A, +1,750 seat B).

## Full-atlas result

Against `bots/common/donothingbot`, seed 1, round 1000, all 53 maps:

- seat A: 1,712,340 Ti;
- seat B: 1,709,610 Ti;
- combined: 3,421,950 Ti;
- improvement over `eitri@cc2fa28`: +48,160 Ti;
- executor: 96% of the selected plan on both seats;
- planner: 93% / 92% of the throughput-aware terrain ceiling.

`tests/test_eitri.py` passes and checks all maps, both Core anchors, and every
builder count from one through four. Both complete engine sweeps finished with
no TLE. The reusable competitive result remains the timing-preserving miner
cadence already ported into Brynhildr; its online scheduling is controlled by
the farm maintainers, not this experiment.
