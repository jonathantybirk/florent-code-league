# Cambridge Battlecode 2026

> **Measured before the Aug 4 turret patch (fcode ≤ 2.3.3).** Everything below was
> measured when turrets were stronger. The 2026-08-04 balance pass (fcode 2.3.4)
> changed the Gunner to 25 HP (was 40), 20 Ti (was 10), +20% cost scaling (was
> +10%), 7 damage (was 10) and 4 ammo per shot (was 2), and the Sentinel to 40 HP
> (was 30) on a 2-round reload (was 3). That balance pass is the only rules change
> in 2.3.4, so conclusions here that do not turn on turret strength still stand —
> but every turret-heavy number needs re-measuring before it is trusted again.

## Statements about the finalists

Leung stated that Pantheon was writing a paper about what he called a “new approach to terrain analysis adapted from StarCraft AI research.” This directory does not identify the details of that method because no Pantheon paper or technical postmortem was located during this pass.

Pantheon member Alex Thummalapalli stated that the team won the Grand Finals and defeated the teams ranked second, third, and fourth. His profile also lists a Cambridge Battlecode “Best Postmortem” award.

Sources: [organizer summary](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/james-leung-dev_5864605-games-played-587-teams-1518-activity-7462172589520662528-SfO5), [Thummalapalli's finals post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alext101_im-thrilled-to-announce-that-my-team-placed-activity-7458231178710556673--A84), [Thummalapalli profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alext101)

Kessoku Band member Mikhail Borbe lists third place in the Grand Finals and first place in the international qualifiers. He described the team's six weeks as repeated testing, iteration, and rebuilding around techniques that worked. No detailed Kessoku Band technical postmortem was located during this pass.

Source: [Mikhail Borbe profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mikhail-borbe)

Something else member Matthew Huang lists second place worldwide. No detailed technical postmortem from something else was located during this pass.

Source: [Matthew Huang profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/the-matthew-huang)
