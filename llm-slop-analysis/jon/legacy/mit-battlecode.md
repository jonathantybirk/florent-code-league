# MIT Battlecode results and participant accounts

> **Measured before the Aug 4 turret patch (fcode ≤ 2.3.3).** Everything below was
> measured when turrets were stronger. The 2026-08-04 balance pass (fcode 2.3.4)
> changed the Gunner to 25 HP (was 40), 20 Ti (was 10), +20% cost scaling (was
> +10%), 7 damage (was 10) and 4 ammo per shot (was 2), and the Sentinel to 40 HP
> (was 30) on a 2-round reload (was 3). That balance pass is the only rules change
> in 2.3.4, so conclusions here that do not turn on turret strength still stand —
> but every turret-heavy number needs re-measuring before it is trusted again.

## Just Woke Up — 2025 winner

The team's published postmortem describes its development chronologically. For Sprint 1, the authors characterized their strategy as crude: soldiers pursued towers and paint, moppers cleaned paint, and splashers spread paint. They wrote that the bot primarily won through map coverage and did not yet attack towers. They reported placing in the top 16 in that sprint and then losing.

The authors later described adjusting resource-pattern overlap with a two-dimensional exclusion array after a patch, changing tower construction following a tower-sacrificing nerf, and encountering map-dependent outcomes when initial tower types were selected with coordinate-seeded pseudo-randomness.

Source: [Just Woke Up 2025 postmortem (PDF)](https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2025-just-woke-up.pdf)

## Gone Fishin' — 2023 runner-up

The official results record Gone Fishin' as the first seed entering the 2023 finals and second overall. The team's postmortem says its code was open-sourced.

Sources: [official past-years page](https://battlecode.org/past.html), [Gone Fishin' postmortem (PDF)](https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2023-gone-fishin.pdf)

## don't @ me — seventh in 2023

The official results record don't @ me in seventh place. Its postmortem describes balancing resource-harvesting assignments, grouping launchers into squadrons, pathfinding and group movement, and kiting behavior.

Sources: [official past-years page](https://battlecode.org/past.html), [don't @ me postmortem (PDF)](https://battlecode.org/assets/files/postmortem-2023-dont-at-me.pdf)
