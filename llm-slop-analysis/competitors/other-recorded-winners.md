## Entelect Challenge 2024

Entelect's finals report records Willie Theron first, Riaan Nel second, and Marvin Thobejane third. It also lists Jesse Bristow fourth, Kobus Van Schoor fifth, Scott Schultz sixth, Jean-Paul De Conceicao seventh, and Aneso Jairosi eighth.

In the same report, Entelect's Tim McFadyen said success was about strategy rather than having the “smartest or fastest” bot, and stated that a simple bot could be effective with the right strategy. That is the organizer representative's analysis.

Source: [Entelect 2024 finals report](https://culture.entelect.co.za/news/2024-entelect-challenge-finals/)
