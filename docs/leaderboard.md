## Readings

**1. "Jon beats Lucas" is not supported by the data.** `lockin` vs `luc1` is **16–14** — a two-game margin,
which sits *inside* the ±2–4 game noise floor established in G/M01 (a control variant that provably could
not lengthen any path still moved 7 games). Head to head, those two bots are **statistically tied**. Any
claim that the anti-Lucas counter-tuning "works" needs a stronger measurement than this pairing provides.

**2. `lockin`'s real edge is elsewhere, and it is genuine.** 100 vs 91 across 120 games is a 9-game gap,
comfortably outside the noise floor. But it does not come from the head-to-head — it comes from cleaner
sweeps of the weaker bots: 30–0 vs frontier where luc1 goes 28–2, and 30–0 vs starter_fixed where luc1
goes 27–3. `lockin` is a fork of `luc1` (~32-line diff), so this is the parent bot plus polish, not a
different strategy.

**3. This is the ladder-relevant ordering, and it inverts the internal narrative.** Against a *random*
field you are graded on total, not on beating one named rival. `lockin` and `luc1` are the trunk
candidates; the counter-tuning is a tiebreaker between two nearly identical bots and would be dead weight
against opponents neither has seen.

**4. AutistimusPrime is third and clearly behind.** 55/120. It beats `frontier` 18–12 and `starter_fixed`
23–7, but loses 8–22 and 6–24 to the top two. The gap is real and is not a measurement artifact.

**5. `frontier` is the weakest real bot.** 38/120, and it loses **0–30** to `lockin` — Jon's own fork of
Lucas's bot beats Jon's own flagship 30–0. That is the strongest single argument in this table for
consolidating on one trunk instead of maintaining separate lineages.

## What this says about picking a trunk

- `lockin` and `luc1` are within a hair of each other and both far ahead of everything else. Either is a
  defensible trunk; `lockin` is `luc1` plus polish, so the merge is nearly free.
- `frontier` should be retired as a lineage and mined for parts (its collector economy was rated the best
  in the repo by the branch audit, even though the bot as a whole is last among the real bots).
- AutistimusPrime's contribution to a merged trunk is not its win rate — it is the ground-truth register,
  the arena harness, the crash/validator gate, the map atlas, and the verified rush plan, none of which
  the other bots have.

## Caveats

- Every bot here is measured on *this* pool of five. Ladder opponents will differ, and counter-tuning
  aimed at a specific teammate does not transfer.
- Nothing here has been measured under enforced CPU limits on Linux. `luc1` reportedly carries a routine
  its author timed at ~21 ms against a 10 ms budget, with the fix left on a different branch. If that is
  real, this entire table could reorder on the actual ladder. **That audit is still unrun.**
